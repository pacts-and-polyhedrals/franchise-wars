# v1.2.1 — Adjustable QuestRabbit Cut
- The QuestRabbit cut is back on the GM Dashboard, as an optional campaign rule.
- **Overview tab:** −5% / +5% buttons (0–30%) set the cut, with how much the party keeps shown alongside.
- **Optional rule: falling commission.** A suggested schedule ties the cut to the Rabbit Rating: ★ 30% · ★★ 25% · ★★★ 20% · ★★★★ 15% · ★★★★★ 10%. With the rule on, the cut drops to that rate automatically when a milestone star is earned, and there's a button to apply the rate for the current rating. You can still set any rate by hand.
- The current cut shows in the summary strip and on the campaign status card, and every change goes in the Activity Log.
- The schedule is a house rule, not in the book; with the rule off, the cut stays at the standard 30%.
- The summary strip now fits on one line.

Includes everything in v1.2.0.
