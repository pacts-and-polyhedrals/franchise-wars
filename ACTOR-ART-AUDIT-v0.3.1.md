## Creature/Bespoke Art Gate

Verified in the current runtime library: **TK-03 Veronique Salt**, **TK-06 Mascot Revenant (Mortimer Pell)** and **TK-09 Tallis Vend, Chairman**. The production record explicitly says the following custom art was generated, but their exact standalone binaries cannot be positively identified among the recovered 300 runtime files: **TK-01 Compliance Officer, TK-02 Senior Auditor, TK-04 The Mother Culture, TK-05 Golden Tusk Construct, TK-07 Shop Steward (Unseen Servant Collective)**. v0.3.1 therefore uses a neutral Foundry placeholder for those five instead of substituting unrelated franchise art.

Across the complete actor audit, the unresolved original custom-art binaries are therefore **six unique assets**: **Auntie Vermillia (CO-10c)** plus **TK-01, TK-02, TK-04, TK-05 and TK-07**.
