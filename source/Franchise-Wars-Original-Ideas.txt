# THE FRANCHISE WARS
## A Corporate Fantasy Campaign of Questionable Employment, Hostile Takeovers & Heroic Customer Service

> **“Adventure is dangerous. Employment is mandatory.”**  
> — QuestRabbit Adventuring Solutions Employee Handbook, Section 1

---

# CAMPAIGN PREMISE

The age of the independent tavern is dying.

Across the kingdoms, corporations have discovered something far more powerful than dragons, archmages or divine favour:

**brand recognition.**

Franchises now dominate the cities.

Coffee is purchased from multinational wizarding conglomerates. Inns operate through enchanted loyalty schemes. Necromancers handle night shifts. Tieflings manage customer complaints. Kobolds run kitchens. Adventurers buy equipment at warehouse stores before accepting contracts from the same corporations whose incompetence created the dungeon they are being paid to enter.

And when something goes wrong?

They hire adventurers.

The player characters are contractors working through **QuestRabbit Adventuring Solutions**, a magical employment agency connecting desperate corporations with expendable heroes.

Every assignment begins with something mundane.

A delivery hasn't arrived.

An employee hasn't returned.

A freezer is making noises.

A rival stole a recipe.

A customer refuses to leave.

Then someone opens the cellar door.

---

# QUESTRABBIT ADVENTURING SOLUTIONS

QuestRabbit operates enchanted job boards throughout the civilized world.

Clients post contracts.

Adventurers accept them.

QuestRabbit takes **30%**.

The adventurers are informed this is an industry-standard rate.

It is not.

### CONTRACT GRADES

**Copper Contracts — Levels 1–4**  
Pest control, deliveries, missing employees, petty corporate espionage.

**Silver Contracts — Levels 5–8**  
Monster nests, sabotage, dangerous acquisitions, supernatural workplace incidents.

**Gold Contracts — Levels 9–12**  
Interplanar procurement, corporate warfare, powerful magical entities.

**Platinum Contracts — Levels 13–16**  
Demonic mergers, dimensional monopolies, ancient franchises and magical catastrophes.

**Black Card Contracts — Levels 17–20**  
Jobs that technically never existed.
