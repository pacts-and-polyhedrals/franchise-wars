# The Franchise Wars v0.5.0 — Mission Guides

All 24 mission journals are now full DM guides with these parts:

- **Contract Cover:** the QuestRabbit job slip (player-safe), with a Send to chat button.
- **DM Overview:** synopsis, background, running notes, campaign threads and the hook read-aloud.
- **Scene 1–5:** each scene page has
  - a battlemap link and the NPCs and creatures present, all linked to their Actors;
  - handouts with Show buttons and read-aloud with Send to chat;
  - the DM description, area features, NPC play notes and a checks table with clickable roll requests;
  - lines to steal, developments, complications and scaling.
- **Opposition & Tactics:** every foe, linked to its Actor and to the scenes it appears in.
- **NPC Dossiers:** appearance, voice, wants, fears, secret, quotes, scenes and later appearances.
- **Handouts:** the mission's player handouts, revealed one page at a time.
- **Twist, Rewards & Aftermath:** the Receipt with an Award & announce button, an outcomes table and a link to the next mission.

GM-only: the Request, Send to chat, Show and Award buttons respond only to the GM.
