# v1.2.0 — The GM Dashboard
One panel replaces the Campaign Tracker, Clause Debate and Board Vote windows and their macros. Open it from the **Franchise Wars GM Dashboard** button at the top of the Journals sidebar, the **FW: GM Dashboard** macro, or Content Manager → Open GM Dashboard.

**Layout**
- A summary strip that is always visible: Act, Rabbit Rating stars, missions complete, clause Receipts held, clauses void, Board seats turned, and Commons allies.
- Eight tabs: Overview · Missions · Leverage · Board Vote · Final Contract · Receipts · Commons Tally · Activity Log.
- Every tab scrolls, keeps its scroll position as things update, and opens with the full rules from the Systems chapter in a collapsible panel.

**Tabs**
- **Overview:** the Rabbit Rating with what each star unlocks and its hidden meaning, a Mandate toggle, the current act, the next mission with its synopsis, and recent activity.
- **Missions:** all 24 missions by act, each with grade, level, client, synopsis, Receipt status, a status selector and an Open guide button.
- **Leverage:** a card per corporation with slogan, goal, public face and who controls it, plus +/− and one-click Spend for Advantage, Access or Cover (whispered to the GM).
- **Board Vote:** each umbrella's director, Leverage and seat; Demand proxy (−2 Leverage); the S23 result, including the Commons Tally modifier; and an announcement card for chat.
- **Final Contract:** the S24 Clause Debate. It shows the clause being read, whether its Receipt is held, the DC (18 or 25), check requests, burn / three witnesses / Ledger Entry, plus the full clause list with undo.
- **Receipts:** all 23 Receipts, the clause each one voids, which character holds it, and award or undo.
- **Commons Tally:** allies with how each was won, the S23 and S24 effects, and the 20-ally Held in Common ending.

**Automatic tracking**
- Giving a Receipt item to any character marks that Receipt held and the mission complete.
- The guides' Award Receipt and Record Leverage buttons update the dashboard.
- Marking S02, S05, S10, S15 or S20 complete adds a Rabbit Rating star, once each and never past five. Completing a mission also moves the current act forward.
- Every Receipt, Leverage change, Board seat, voided clause, ally, mission change and handout sent to chat is written to the Activity Log.
- The Grey Creep counter and the changeable QuestRabbit cut are gone (neither is in the rules). The cut is now the fixed 30%.

Includes everything in v1.1.5.
